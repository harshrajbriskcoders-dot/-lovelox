The locket guide

Questions, answered.

Read the journal
What is a locket necklace?

A pendant that opens on a small hinge, like a book, to hold something precious inside — today, usually two photographs. Worn on a chain, it rests close to the heart, which is why lockets have long marked love, loyalty and remembrance, and are so often passed down as heirlooms.

What are LOVELOX lockets made of?

925 sterling silver is the foundation of our lockets. Gold and rose gold lockets, and their chains, are 925 sterling silver with 18ct gold or 18ct rose gold plate. A few designs are also offered in platinum-plated brass — each product page lists its exact metal.

How do my photographs go inside?

Upload two photos when you create your locket and preview them in place. We size each to the locket's template, print it on premium glossy photo paper, cut it to shape and set it by hand beneath a clear protective film.

What can I have engraved?

Initials, a name, a date or a short message, engraved with a diamond-tipped tool. Choose Times, Sans-Serif or Script, across up to three lines of around ten characters each, depending on the locket.

Which chain length should I choose?

Necklaces adjust to 16 or 18 inches as standard. For a longer drop, choose the chain that adjusts to 20, 22 or 24 inches.

Can jewellery hold a loved one's ashes?

Yes, in two ways. Resin pieces have ashes delicately set in resin within the design; urn pieces have a discreet compartment you fill yourself, with the tools we provide.
